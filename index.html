Dr. Pavan Kumar M.P. is an Associate Professor in the Department of Information Science and Engineering at Jawaharlal Nehru National College of Engineering, Shivamogga, Karnataka. He has more than 16 years of teaching and research experience in Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, and Data Analytics.

Research Interests

Artificial Intelligence, Long-Range Human Recognition, Medical Image Enhancement, Behavioural & Cognitive Analytics, Biodiversity and Disease Surveillance.

Academic & Professional Roles

IEEE Reviewer, Organizing Chair (IEEE CS Events), VTU BOS & BOE Member, Resource Person for VTU Workshops, COE Proposal Lead (STIIA).
